PDPM Calculator & IPA Decision Tool

FY 2027 Medicare SNF PPS rates (Oct 1, 2026 – Sep 30, 2027) · Component groups, CMIs, HIPPS, per diem, variable per diem schedule, and 5-Day vs IPA comparison

Sources & method. FY 2027 SNF PPS Final Rule (CMS-1843-F): unadjusted federal per diem rates, PDPM case-mix indexes (identical urban/rural), labor-related share 72.0%. Per diem = Σ(component rate × CMI × variable per diem factor) × (labor share × wage index + non-labor share) × VBP multiplier. PT/OT factor 1.00 days 1–20, then −2% every 7 days (0.76 floor by day 98); NTA ×3.0 days 1–3, ×1.0 after. Nursing component +18% for HIV/AIDS. Function-score averages are not rounded. Nursing classification follows the RAI Manual Chapter 6 category hierarchy (Extensive Services → Special Care High → Special Care Low → Clinically Complex → Behavioral/Cognitive → Reduced Physical Function). Clinical category must be chosen from the I0020B primary-diagnosis mapping. This is a planning tool: confirm final groups with your MDS grouper (PCC / SimpleLTC) before billing. State/county wage indexes come from the CMS FY 2027 SNF county-level wage index file (CMS-1843-F); values are before the provider-level 5% cap on decreases, so override the wage index if your building's capped value differs.
